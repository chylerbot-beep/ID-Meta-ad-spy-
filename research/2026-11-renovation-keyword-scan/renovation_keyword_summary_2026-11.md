# Singapore renovation keyword ad scan — 18 November 2026

## Coverage and interpretation

This is a **partial, directional sample**, not a complete picture of Singapore's Meta ads. Searches used Singapore, All ads and active status on 18 November 2026. The CSV contains distinct ads observed in the first results across the scan; many searches were not exhausted and most have fewer than the requested approximately 10 relevant new ads. The ad-level entries note unverified formats, scopes and creative deduplication. Exact library IDs were checked against the 100-firm CSV and September swipe file; similarity of creative is not fully verified. Results were not sampled randomly; result order, duplicate removal and search noise prevent reliable market-share or frequency estimates.

The searches "landed property", "bungalow", "semi-d", "terrace house", "A&A works", "addition and alteration" and "reconstruction" produced no new eligible Singapore renovation ads in the initial results reviewed; many visible hits were unrelated property listings, Malaysian advertisers or irrelevant results. This does **not** mean no such advertisers exist. Earlier BTO, HDB, resale and condo searches were also shallow. Ads may stop or offers expire after observation. A 60+ day run is a longevity signal only, not proof of performance or continuous delivery.

## BTO

Observed advertisers include Artispace, ID.eology, RS Carpentry, GoDesign, Proz Design and Monarch. Artispace advertised 3/4/5-room BTO packages at S$21,888/22,888/23,888 plus a spin-and-win promotion; GoDesign advertised S$7,860/8,260/8,660, and Monarch advertised S$6,888/7,888/8,888. ID.eology advertised from S$6,888 and RS Carpentry from S$7,288. These are **advertised entry prices, not comparable full-home quotes**: detailed inclusions, exclusions and current eligibility are not established.

Hooks include collecting keys, low entry price, direct or in-house build, free gifts, and fear of hidden charges. Several observed examples use video; some formats remain unverified. Quote and WhatsApp CTAs recur in the sample. Woodates can show its S$32,888 four-room package alongside a concrete inclusion list, scope limits and a real finished home. Explain what the price covers and document variation-order rules.

## Resale HDB

Observed advertisers include Stoneridge, ID.eology, D&I Design, Monarch, GoDesign, Reno Scout and i-Plan. ID.eology advertised from S$29,399; GoDesign advertised S$32,060 for an unspecified-size resale package. D&I advertised kitchen plus two bathrooms at S$27,388 nett with E0 plywood or S$28,688 nett with aluminium, with 12-month warranty claimed. Monarch advertised 3/4/5-room resale S$18,888/19,888/21,888 and EA S$23,888; its headline separately listed S$20,888 for 5-room, so clarification is needed. Scope and conditions for all offers remain unverified.

Stoneridge's long-running ad claims 40% or S$20k–S$40k savings, zero variation orders and a no-delay guarantee. Those are advertiser claims, not independently verified. Before-and-after, defects, cost certainty and coordinated trades are useful hooks. Woodates could lead with a **site inspection and itemised scope** covering demolition, waterproofing, electrical and plumbing assumptions; show an actual resale project and explain how unforeseen defects are handled. Avoid promising zero variations before inspecting an old flat.

## Condo

Observed advertisers include ChengYi, Reno Scout, IdeasXchange and i-Plan. Home tours and design-led videos show the finished outcome, while IdeasXchange emphasises compact layouts and storage. Monarch lists new-condo essential prices of S$6,588/7,088/8,588 for 2/3/4 rooms; the scope is unverified. Other condo ads in this small sample do not establish a typical full-renovation price.

Woodates could offer a condo consultation focused on management rules, permitted work hours, protection of common areas, storage and practical carpentry, supported by a real project and a clear itemised estimate. Avoid equating an entry-level carpentry price with a complete condo renovation.

## Landed and A&A

The keyword sample is particularly weak here. ARL advertises bespoke design and carpentry across landed and other property types; Cove advertises financing at 0.9% interest with rate basis and conditions unverified. No new dedicated Singapore landed rebuild or A&A specialist was established from the initial results of the more specific searches. There is **not enough evidence** to identify dominant landed advertisers, price points, formats or a reliable market gap from this keyword sample.

If Woodates enters this segment, start with clearly defined interior renovation or carpentry work, use real relevant projects and an inspection-led estimate, and explain which work requires qualified professionals, approvals or specialist partners. Avoid advertising structural A&A or reconstruction capability that Woodates cannot substantiate or deliver, quoting a fixed whole-house price without scope, and implying accreditation it does not hold.

## What Woodates should do and avoid

Use the own carpentry factory, in-house trades, HDB licence since 2018, 24-month warranty and 5.0 Google rating as differentiators **only where accurate and current**, with warranty scope and rating context shown. The sample shows other advertisers also use in-house and factory claims, so demonstrate Woodates' actual factory, people and completed work rather than relying on the words alone.

Avoid unsubstantiated No.1, lowest-price or guaranteed-savings claims; drip pricing; inflated or unverifiable gift valuations; and any implication Woodates is CaseTrust accredited. Do not present long-running ads as confirmed winners. Keep package scope, exclusions, GST treatment, financing terms and conditions understandable. For resale and landed, do not compare an uninspected project's price to a tightly scoped entry package.

## Useful next steps

Expand each keyword beyond initial results to reach approximately 10 eligible new ads where available, especially Singapore landed and A&A contractors. Recheck creative similarity, watch the videos to classify formats, and record CTA destinations without clicking ad CTAs. Revisit earlier BTO, HDB, resale and condo terms as their samples are also incomplete. Update this summary only when broader coverage supports stronger conclusions.
