# Singapore interior design competitor ad research — November 2026

## Scope and reading the evidence

The companion [CSV](competitor_ads_2026-11.csv) records the first 100 listings in the supplied Google-review list, using Meta Ad Library's Singapore, All ads, active filters, observed 16–18 November 2026. Each listing has at least one record. Listings 24–27 were revisited after the earlier failed advertiser searches. The work is a directional sample, not a complete census: several rows use keyword results or only a likely main page; alternate advertiser pages, branch-specific pages, creative details and selection of the five oldest ads are not comprehensively verified. “No active ads” means none appeared on the specified page under those filters at the observation time, not that the company runs no ads anywhere. Six listing identities remain unverified: Ascend Design, D&R Design Reno, Visionary Interior Design, 1618 Studio, M2 Decor and Greeen. Treat ambiguous page matches such as firm 63 with caution.

Some source-list branches share the same advertiser and ad IDs, notably Design 4 Space and Starry Homestead. They are not independent campaigns. Count each Library ID once for campaign analysis; Meta may also group multiple ads under one creative. Counts are approximate, and the CSV contains recruitment and service ads as well as homeowner acquisition ads.

Days in the CSV are calendar days from Meta's displayed start date to the observation date. They do **not** establish uninterrupted delivery, spend, reach, leads, conversion or performance. An ad can remain listed as active while containing an old seasonal promotion; check the current destination and terms before using any price. Competitor credentials and promises below are claims in their ads, not independently audited facts.

## Repeated offers and messages

The observed ads repeatedly use free consultations or quotations, low “from” prices, itemised packages, urgency, free appliances or upgrades, event perks and lucky draws. Examples include [WHST's S$6,688 kitchen with advertised appliance gifts](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=779947878152316), [Ft2's free whole-house 3D preview](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2043372943040149), and [Starry Homestead's anniversary promotion](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2212322609635740). These are recurring patterns in the sample; the sampling limitations do not support a market-wide prevalence estimate. Claimed gift values, scarcity and guarantee wording require their own conditions and evidence.

Trust messages also compete directly: [Reborn](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1987548481996696) advertises no hidden costs or delays; [HOFT](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=952023694628073) offers a 90-minute discussion and a space plan and moodboard; [MYD](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=3392369081151197) advertises an in-house factory and trades alongside credentials; [Swiss Interior](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2237361843859088) advertises CaseTrust, completed homes and after-sales support. These are competitors' claims, not endorsements or independently verified credentials.

## Creative and audience patterns

- **BTO and value-conscious HDB:** package grids, room counts, key collection and first-renovation questions; quote or WhatsApp lead capture. [LOME](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1686559102887999) and [Mr Shopper Studio's BTO planning ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1174004371969835) illustrate different price and education approaches. The latter's permit statements are its claims, not legal guidance.
- **Resale and practical renovation:** kitchen and bathroom bundles, itemised work, waterproofing, storage and before/after projects. [REGO](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=975090765426688) and [SEIJI's Ryokan resale project](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1755030099057524) show both offer-led and project-led approaches.
- **Condo and landed:** more lifestyle, material and design-process stories. [Mr Shopper Studio's condo ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1091347227273769) sells a hotel-inspired feeling; its [landed ad](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=2285089336221530) discusses existing conditions and planning.
- **Aesthetic and lifestyle projects:** [Forefront's Japandi project](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=791563306962948), [SEIJI's wabi-sabi resale](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1094631430208875), and [Brickwood's connected living spaces](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1148195941004290) lead with a look or home story without a visible package price. Aesthetic-led does not mean the advertiser has no offers elsewhere.
- **Practical education:** [Dots 'n' Tots](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=4665249403757072) explains socket planning; [Zenith Arc](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/?id=1819049756084217) shows compact HDB tips. These can build relevance before asking for a quote.

Video is common in the captured sample, alongside carousels, images and formats that could not be determined. Observed calls to action include Get Quote, WhatsApp or message, Book Now, Learn More, Instagram and forms. These observations describe creative choices, not measured effectiveness.

## Positioning for Woodates

The supplied brief describes Woodates' own factory, in-house trades, 24-month warranty, HDB licence since 2018 and Google 5.0 rating. Use these only after checking current evidence, the precise entities involved and warranty terms. Woodates is **not** CaseTrust accredited; do not imply it is, use a CaseTrust mark, or describe HDB licensing as an equivalent certification. A practical proposition for a roughly S$30,000 BTO couple is a documented scope, visible production and accountable aftercare, paired with an achievable design. Factory ownership and transparent quotations are not unique in this sample, so show the actual proof rather than assert exclusivity.

Ten testable concepts:

1. **“What S$32,888 covers”** — show a 4-room scope card with quantities, exclusions, GST and conditions; CTA: request the itemised package.
2. **Keys-to-plan walkthrough** — a BTO couple's first decisions, from floor plan to a realistic written scope; CTA: book a planning conversation.
3. **Factory to flat** — show actual cabinet production and installation by the relevant Woodates team; explain who does what; CTA: see the process.
4. **One change, one written variation** — illustrate a real example of a requested change and its agreed cost, if Woodates' actual process supports it; CTA: see a sample quote.
5. **Storage that earns its space** — demonstrate three measured solutions in a 4-room BTO and show the price or scope assumptions; CTA: discuss your floor plan.
6. **24-month aftercare explained** — show what the warranty covers, exclusions and how a homeowner requests help; CTA: read warranty terms.
7. **A real S$30k budget conversation** — show trade-offs between carpentry, electrical and finishing with documented project figures and consent; CTA: plan a budget.
8. **HDB credentials made useful** — state the precise licence and its current status, and explain which work requires appropriate approvals without overclaiming; CTA: ask about your scope.
9. **A homeowner's lived-in tour** — a consented client explains a practical design choice and experience; substantiate any review, score or testimonial and avoid implying typical results.
10. **Two looks, same clear scope** — show warm minimalist and modern alternatives with the same specified base package; disclose any finish or upgrade price differences.

Avoid invented discounts, inflated gift values, false scarcity, guaranteed completion or “no hidden costs” claims that cannot be substantiated. Do not imply every home qualifies for a headline price. Explain unavoidable site-dependent work and obtain written consent for variations; avoid drip pricing. Validate all figures, reviews and permissions before running creative.
